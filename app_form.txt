========  What's your connection to your region of choice? (200-words max) 

i began in chicago--it's where i'm from originally and continue to visit often. that said, i consider los angeles to be my 'comedy home' since i spent some very formative stage years there and have great affection for that town. like many LA comedians in 2020, the strict lockdowns sent me looking for an alternative comedy scene. i came to fort lauderdale for what i thought would be a temporary visit but, what can i say, florida won me over :) so i'm still here today. when people make crazy generalizations about how bizaare floridians are, i don't get offended...i just think, "yup, those freaks are my people!"
